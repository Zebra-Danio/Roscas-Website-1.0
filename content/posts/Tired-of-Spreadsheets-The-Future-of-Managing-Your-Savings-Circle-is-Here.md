If you’ve ever had the honour of organising a community savings circle — whether you call it a Paluwagan, Ajo, Esusu, Pardna, Chama, or by another name passed down in your community — you know the unique rewards and challenges that come with the role. The joy of seeing a member receive their “pot” to achieve a dream is immense. But let’s be honest, the administrative work behind the scenes can be draining.

For years, dedicated organisers have used the tools available to them: dog-eared notebooks, complex spreadsheets, and endlessly long WhatsApp or Messenger threads. This ingenuity is a testament to the commitment that holds these circles together. But these tools were never designed for the specific needs of a ROSCA, and the hidden costs of using them can be high.

## The Hidden Costs of Manual Management

• The Admin Overload: Hours spent checking bank transfers, sending “gentle reminders” for payments, updating spreadsheets, and answering the same questions from members: “Has everyone paid yet?” “Whose turn is it next month?”

• The Risk of Confusion: A single typo in a spreadsheet or a missed message in a long chat history can create confusion and, in the worst cases, lead to mistrust or disputes that strain the community bonds you’ve worked so hard to build.

• The Transparency Challenge: It’s difficult for every member to have a clear, real-time view of the circle’s status. This lack of visibility can create uncertainty and requires every member to place blind faith in the organiser’s manual records.

## There Is a Better Way

The power and wisdom of the ROSCA concept deserve a tool that is just as smart and efficient. What if there was a tool designed specifically for your savings circle? A tool that…

• …showed everyone the status of contributions in real-time?

• …sent out payment reminders automatically?

• …displayed a clear and transparent payout schedule for all to see?

• …was simple, secure, and free for everyone in the group to use?

That’s why we’re building RoscasApp.

We believe that the person organising the circle should be able to enjoy the community aspect without being bogged down by the administrative burden. We’ve built a modern, simple, and free mobile application designed to solve these exact problems — and we’re now testing it with a small cohort of organisers running real circles.

RoscasApp handles the tedious admin work, so you can focus on what you do best: leading, supporting, and celebrating with your community.

The future of community savings management is about combining timeless tradition with modern simplicity. And it’s already taking shape.
